# San José AI Permit Review: AI for Faster City Building Permit Review

Client: City of San José, Building Permit Review  
Team: matthew throne, Gabrielle Yang, Frank Leamy, Jet Lau, Anthony Trujillo, Aug–Oct 2026  
My part: I tested the permit-review prototype, created sample permits, and analyzed its risks and feasibility.

## The problem

City staff and residents may wait a long time for building permit feedback. Missing or incorrect information can cause delays because permits must be reviewed by the appropriate city experts.

## What we built

- A prototype that reviews permit documents and identifies possible missing or incorrect information.
- The user receives feedback explaining what may need to be corrected.
- A human city reviewer still makes the final decision, especially for safety-related issues.

## What I would do next

One important gap is that the prototype cannot reliably verify safety-related information. Next, I would build stronger human-review steps and learn more about AI document processing and city permit requirements.
